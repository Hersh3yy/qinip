New this month

Four new tricks

Pick many songs

Cmd+A picks all, Backspace clears them from the list. Your files stay safe.

Folder labels

Drop two folders and each gets its own header, so you know where songs came from.

Volume slider

Right next to the play buttons, with a mute button. Qinip remembers it.

Free for real

Anyone may read, share and change its recipe. Programmers call this the GPL licence.

The licence part is less flashy but important: Qinip uses a free beat-finding tool called aubio, and its rule is that anything built with it must be free and open too. Ask Nami: is it fair that a free tool asks you to share in return?
