How fast is the beat?

BEATS PER MINUTE

121.9

Programmers call it BPM. Qinip listens for sudden jumps in loudness, like drum hits, and counts how often they come.

THE MATHS, CHECK IT

One beat = 60 ÷ 121.9 = 0.492 s

A 4-beat loop = 4 × 0.492 = 1.97 s

That is how the 2, 4, 8 and 16 loop buttons know where to jump back.

The listener also gives its own sureness score: 0.26 out of 1 for this song. Not very sure! So a DJ still checks with their ears.
