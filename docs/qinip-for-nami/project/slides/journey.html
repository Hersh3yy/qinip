One song's trip

STOP 1

The file

An MP3 is sound squashed small, like a zip file.

STOP 2

Unpacker

Turns it back into millions of numbers.

STOP 3

Listener

Finds the beat speed and the key.

STOP 4

Painter

Draws the coloured wave in 800 slices.

STOP 5

Speaker

Plays it, with your knob settings mixed in.

No AI anywhere. Every stop is plain maths and rules, so it gives the same answer every time.
