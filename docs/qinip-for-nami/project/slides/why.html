Why it exists

Hiren's old music player stopped working

The old way

Clementine, the free player he used, broke on the new Apple chips. The replacement, Strawberry, wants €10 a month.

The Qinip way

Build his own. Free, for Mac and Windows, made for DJs who listen to whole folders of new songs.

The name: Qinip is how Hiren says guinep, a Caribbean fruit that grows in bunches.

Hiren got tired of a broken free app and a paid one, so he built his own. Ask Nami: if your favourite app suddenly cost money every month, would you pay or build your own?
