Stop 4, the painter

Four colours, four kinds of sound

Low sounds wobble the air slowly, high sounds fast. Qinip splits every song into four groups, like a choir's bass, tenor, alto and soprano. This is how our test song is split:

Low under 250 Hz

63.2%

LowMid ~1,000 Hz

16.7%

HiMid ~4,000 Hz

9.7%

Hi over 6,000 Hz

10.3%

Hz means wobbles per second. Each EQ knob turns one colour up or down, and you see that colour get brighter or fainter on the waveform. Game: which colour holds the kick drum? (Low, orange.) And the tsss of a cymbal? (Hi, blue.)
