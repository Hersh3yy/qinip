Giving it to strangers

Why would anyone trust a download?

Mac: a passport stamp

Apple scans the app and stamps it, like a bag check at the airport. Without the stamp, a Mac refuses to open it.

Called signing and notarization

Windows: earn reviews

A signature proves who made it. Windows still warns until enough people download it, like a new shop with no reviews yet.

Called Authenticode and SmartScreen

Updates: just ask

Each new version gets a number like 1.1.0. Qinip will ask Hiren's website "anything newer?" and tell you.

Version numbers follow SemVer
