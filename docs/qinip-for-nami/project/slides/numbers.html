Stop 2, the unpacker

To a computer, a song is a list of numbers

44,100

numbers every second, for each ear. Each one says how far to push the speaker.

255.1 s

the length of our test song, ZHU's "Cocaine Model".

22.5 M

numbers in that one song: 22,498,560 to be exact.

Like a flipbook: a cartoon shows 24 pictures a second, a song shows 44,100 tiny speaker positions. (Unlike a flipbook, you hear it, you don't see it.)

Sound is just a long list of numbers. Ask Nami: 44,100 times 255 seconds times 2 ears, roughly how many numbers is that? (About 22.5 million.)
