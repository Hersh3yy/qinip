Your turn

Beat the listener

Pick a song you love. Tap along to the beat for 15 seconds and count your taps. Times 4 gives your BPM.

Then load it in Qinip. Who got closer, you or the listener?

Example: 30 taps in 15 seconds, times 4, is 120 BPM. Let Nami do the maths herself and then check it in Qinip together.
